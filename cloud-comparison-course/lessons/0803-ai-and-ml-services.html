Nothing is missing from the AI rows, so the design question is what counts as a product

working 7 min The six capabilities of the ai-ml area, answered by AWS, Azure, Google Cloud and OCI in that order.

The one-minute version

  • Six rows, twenty-four cells, and not one recorded absence. This is the only area in the module where every cloud answers everything.
  • The difference is where each cloud draws the line around a product: a purpose-built service, or a feature of something you already run.
  • The one cell that is not a service is the module's clearest delivered-elsewhere: OCI's retrieval pipeline lives inside its agent service, and the cell links there.
  • Three of the four professional blueprints name no AI domain at all, and on the fourth the AI content sits in an unscored block. Design for the workload, not for the marks.

1. Six capabilities, one stack

Two rows are settled and worth naming once. Hosted inference is Amazon Bedrock, Microsoft Foundry Models, Vertex AI and the OCI Generative AI service. Train, tune and deploy is SageMaker AI, Azure Machine Learning, Vertex AI training and pipelines and the OCI Data Science service. Four names, one row, and the shape behind each is the same.

The vector row is the sharpest. AWS and Google Cloud sell purpose-built vector services, Azure adds a vector index to a search service or a database you were already running, and OCI has no standalone vector product at all: the VECTOR datatype and its similarity operators make Autonomous Database the vector store. The same instinct runs in the opposite direction on the pretrained row, where OCI ships Speech, Vision, Language and Document Understanding as four named services and the other three bundle the family behind one row entry.

A badge on a service name is doing real work in this area. Two of the four agent platforms and one of the four retrieval pipelines are marked preview in the matrix, and one AWS pair on the agent row is marked retiring: Amazon Q Business and Amazon Kendra are in maintenance mode and closed to new customers, with Kendra workloads pointed at Bedrock Knowledge Bases. A badge is what tells you which of two services in one cell a new design should pick, and this is the area where it appears most.

3. The one cell that is not a service

That single cell is worth dwelling on because it is the state this whole course exists to protect. The matrix says the capability is present, names the service it is inside, and links the row that service holds. A comparison that counted product names would have written OCI down as having no managed retrieval pipeline, which is false, and a design built on that mistake would have rebuilt something already bought.

5. What this certification tests

Very little, and saying so is more useful than pretending otherwise. None of the four professional blueprints carries an AI domain. AZ-305 names identity and governance, data storage, business continuity and infrastructure. 1Z0-997-26 names high availability, cloud-native, security, databases and observability. The Professional Cloud Architect guide has six design and operations domains and names its AI products inside them rather than giving them one. And SAP-C02 puts its AI content in an explicit emerging-topics block that is pretest-only and unscored, naming guardrails, agent access controls and human-oversight workflows.

Retrieval practice

Answer from memory before revealing.

A team needs approximate nearest-neighbour search over embeddings on OCI. What does the matrix record?
The OCI vector cell is a service, and the service is Autonomous Database with its VECTOR datatype and similarity operators. The first invents an absence in a row that has none. The second gets the state right and the packaging wrong, which is the difference this page is about. The fourth is the state of the neighbouring retrieval row on OCI, not this one, and mixing the two loses the distinction the matrix is drawing.
Which is the strongest reason to prefer a purpose-built vector service over a vector index in a store you already run?
The area records no absence and no ranking, so the choice rests on facts this page does not carry, such as the scale and the operational cost on your own workload. The second dresses a packaging observation up as a reason. The third is false: every cell in the row answers the capability, whatever it is packaged inside. The fourth reads a judgement into a table that only records what exists and links it.

Practice about 15 minutes, with paper

Practice 1working

You are asked for a one-page portability note: "a grounded question-and-answer service, and what moves if we change cloud". The design is the sequence diagram above.

List the five participants and, for each, say whether all four clouds answer it with a service. Then identify the two participants whose move costs most, and state the reason in each case in a single sentence that would survive a fact check.

Hint

Read the six rows of the area first and note which one is not a service on one cloud. Then ask which participants are load-bearing when you cross a boundary: the ones whose data has to come with them.

Hint on the second part

One of the two is expensive because the capability is packaged inside something larger on one cloud. The other is expensive for a reason that has nothing to do with packaging and everything to do with what is stored.

Show the worked solution
  1. Caller. Yours, not the platform's. It moves with the application.
  2. Agent runtime. All four answer the agent row with a service. Two of the four are marked preview, so the interface you write against on those two is the one likeliest to change under you.
  3. Retrieval pipeline. Three of four sell it separately. On OCI it is delivered inside Generative AI Agents, so there is no separate pipeline to lift out or to point another caller at.
  4. Vector index. All four answer the row. AWS and Google Cloud sell a vector service, Azure indexes vectors in a search service or a database, and on OCI the database is the vector store.
  5. Model API. All four answer the row with hosted inference, and all four resell third-party frontier models, while three of them also serve a first-party family of their own.
  6. Pick the two expensive moves. The retrieval pipeline, because on one cloud it has no separate existence, so moving to or from OCI is a repackaging rather than a swap. And the vector index, because moving it means re-embedding and re-indexing the corpus, which is work proportional to the data rather than to the code.

Sanity check. Your note should contain no sentence of the form "cloud X has no Y" for any row in this area, because the area records no absences at all. If one crept in, check whether you meant "X does not sell Y separately", which is a different and much smaller claim.

Your teacher. When a vendor announcement lands and you want to know whether it changes your design, ask which capability key it answers before you ask what it is called. A new brand on an existing row is a rename, and it changes nothing. A brand that answers a row the cloud had no service for is the one to read properly, and in this area there are none left to fill.
