# MISSION - Inside Azure

## Why this course exists

Microsoft Azure is documented service by service, in its own vocabulary, on thousands of
pages. Nothing walks an experienced engineer through the platform *as one connected
design*: how the account hierarchy, the network fabric, the identity model and the
services that sit on them compose into something you can reason about. This course
is that walk, end to end, at the depth a solution architect needs.

It is one of four sibling per-cloud courses (Azure here) under the Cloud
Architecture category, written to a shared capability taxonomy so that any page here
has a named counterpart in [Comparing the Four Clouds](../cloud-comparison-course/index.html).

## Who it is for

An engineer or architect who is new to Azure but not to cloud computing: virtual
machines, VPC-equivalent networking, IAM concepts and managed databases are assumed
known as *ideas*; everything specific to Azure is taught. Ladder:

- **foundation** (`pill easy`) - the orientation pages: regions, the account
  hierarchy, the shape of the platform.
- **working** (`pill med`) - most pages: one mechanism per page, mechanism first,
  trade-offs after.
- **frontier** (`pill hard`) - reserved for the pages where even the vendor's
  guidance is thin.

## What done looks like

After the course the learner can design a real workload on Azure: place it,
network it, identity it, observe it, survive losing an availability zone, and pay
for it knowingly - and say which decisions are Azure-specific rather than generic.

## What is out of scope

- **Cross-cloud comparison.** The comparison course owns "all four side by side".
  This page links out when a contrast teaches, and otherwise stays in-platform.
- **Unverified claims.** Pages are written only from verified, sourced research;
  until a topic's research is verified, it stays unwritten.
- **Certification preparation as such.** No exam drills, dumps, study calendars or
  time-bound material. The audience includes exam candidates; the pages stay
  evergreen.
- **Pricing tables.** Cost *shape* is taught; list prices link to the vendor.

## Shape and canon

The canon is Microsoft Azure's own documentation - https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/azure/ - linked from the sentence
that rests on it. Provenance discipline lives in `RESOURCES.md`.
