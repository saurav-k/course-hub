# AGENTS.md - The Staff AI Engineer

Instructions for AI coding agents working inside this course folder.
The repository root [`../AGENTS.md`](../AGENTS.md) still binds, and it wins wherever the two disagree.
This file adds only what is true of this course and nowhere else.

## What this course is

Nine decision chapters for a Staff or Principal engineer who is accountable for an AI system they did not train.
It is the **decision track**, beside `ai-system-design-course` (the interview track) and `agent-engineering-course` (the build track).
It owns one row of the hub that no sibling owns: *should we do this, what will it cost us, how will we know we were wrong, and who has to agree.*

[`MISSION.md`](MISSION.md) carries the full statement, including the table of what the other courses own.

## Read before you write

In this order:

1. [`MISSION.md`](MISSION.md) - why the course exists, the five-field spine, and what is out of scope with an owner for every item. Canonical; do not rewrite it as a side effect of other work.
2. [`NOTES.md`](NOTES.md) - cadence, what this reader will not tolerate, and the gotchas the first author paid for.
3. [`BUILDER-SPEC.md`](BUILDER-SPEC.md) - the delta from the house standard, which is mostly the five-field grid.
4. [`RESOURCES.md`](RESOURCES.md) - the sources this course trusts, with the caveat each one carries. Add anything new there before citing it, and record anything you could not source under `## Gaps`.
5. [`PLOT.md`](PLOT.md) - the true reading order and the dependency edge on every chapter.
6. Two neighbouring chapters, to match voice, depth and structure.

## The rules that bite hardest here

- **This course teaches no mechanism.** Not one paragraph on how a thing works. Name it in a sentence and link to the hub course that owns it. `MISSION.md` has the table of who owns what.
- **The five fields are structural, not a template.** Every decision carries all five, in order, and a topic that cannot be written with all five is the wrong topic. The fourth field, *how you find out you were wrong*, never shrinks to a sentence.
- **Every number arrives with its harness, its sample and the author's own statement of what it does not show.** A number that cannot carry those is cut rather than hedged. See the honesty notes in [`NOTES.md`](NOTES.md).
- **Two sources go stale in weeks.** Anthropic's model deprecation page and OpenAI's deprecations page are the whole evidence base of chapter 0007. Re-fetch both before editing that chapter, or it becomes the first page in the hub to state something provably false.

## Out of scope here

Mechanism of every kind, vendor comparison tables, role and salary data, and the substance of the EU AI Act.
[`MISSION.md`](MISSION.md) owns the full list and names the neighbour that owns each item.

## Maintaining this file

Keep this file for knowledge useful to almost every future agent session in this course.
Do not repeat what the other course files already show; point to the authoritative file instead.
Prefer rewriting or pruning existing entries over appending new ones.
