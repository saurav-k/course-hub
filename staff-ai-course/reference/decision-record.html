The Decision Record

1 templateprint-friendly The five fields every topic in this course is answered in, as the artefact you take into the room. Print it, fill it by hand, and note which field was hard.

The one-minute version

  • Five fields, always in this order: the call, what you must know first, the bill, how you find out you were wrong, and the reversal.
  • A topic that cannot be written with all five is the wrong topic, and the field that would not fill is the finding.
  • The fourth field is the one nothing else in the field teaches. If it reduces to "the metric moves", it has not been written.
  • The fifth enforces honesty: a decision whose reversal is unbounded gets argued differently from one you can back out of on Friday.

The template

FieldWhat it must containThe question that catches an empty one
The call The decision in one sentence, phrased so that a reasonable person could choose either way. Could somebody sensible pick the other option? If not, this is a topic wearing a decision's clothes.
What you must know first The evidence needed before the call can be made, what it costs to get, and where the mechanism is taught. Which number would change my mind, and do I have it? If nothing would, the decision was already made.
The bill Money, latency and team attention, including the recurring part and who pays it. What does this cost every month after launch, and whose headcount is it in?
How you find out you were wrong The signal, the lag before it appears, and who sees it first. All three. If this is wrong, what changes, when, and on whose screen? "The metric moves" is not an answer.
The reversal What undoing it costs, and the point after which you cannot. What exactly cannot be taken back, and when does that start being true?
The reframing The order is fixed and nothing is merged, because the value of the record is comparison. A reader who can put the retrieval decision and the self-hosting decision side by side and read their fourth fields together is holding something no survey of the field can give them.

A worked example

One decision, filled in. It is the one from chapter 7, because it is the hardest field set in the course to fill honestly.

FieldFilled
The call The support assistant reads customer email, queries the customer database and can send replies. We either remove one of those three or we accept an exploitable system.
What you must know first Eight published defences against indirect prompt injection were all bypassed by adaptive attacks above 50 percent attack success, so a filter is not a boundary. A capability layer separating control flow from untrusted data solved 77 percent of a benchmark's tasks with provable security against 84 percent undefended.
The bill Removing outbound send costs the "reply automatically" feature and roughly a third of the projected deflection. Removing the database costs personalisation. Removing email means there is no product. Egress is cheapest and it is a rendering and content-security-policy change, not a model change.
How you find out you were wrong Signal: an external researcher's disclosure, or a customer noticing their data in somebody else's ticket. Lag: unbounded, because nothing in monitoring fires. Seen first by: not us. The comparable public case is scored critical with no user interaction required.
The reversal None worth the name. Re-attaching a leg later is a design change through every layer that touched the removed one, and every product decision made in the meantime assumed it was gone. Decide before launch.

How to use it in a review

  • Fill it before the meeting, not during. The record is the argument; the meeting is where it is tested.
  • Take it to the likeliest dissenter first. That is where you learn the record is wrong while changing it is still free, per chapter 9.
  • Record which field was hard. A blank fourth field is the most useful output this page produces, because it means nobody knows how this decision fails.
  • Keep the rejected alternative. A record without one is an implementation plan, and its own author's test says the program would have been the better artefact.
  • Re-read it in eighteen months. The successor should be able to act on it without finding you. If a section summarises what you decided rather than why the alternative lost, it will not survive your departure.
Your teacher. Fill this in for a decision your team has already made and shipped, not one that is open. The retrospective version is uncomfortable and fast, and it usually reveals that the fourth field was never answered for a system that has been in production for a year.
