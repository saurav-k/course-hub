THE STAFF AI ENGINEER Glossary Course Hub
For the engineer who signs the decision, not the pull request

Knowing how it works is not the job.

Deciding, pricing, and being able to say how you will find out you were wrong is the job. This course teaches no mechanism at all: every topic is a decision, and every mechanism question links out to the hub course that already owns it. Nine chapters, fifty decisions, each answered in the same five fields.

The third track beside the interview track and the build track. Interview track: what you would say. Build track: what you would put in the repository. This one: what you put your name on. Start with Chapter 1 and keep the decision record open when a real call is in front of you.

Considered and deliberately not reserved

  • ComplianceThe EU AI Act was researched for its timeline and not its substance. A chapter needs its own research round before a position is claimed for it.
  • Retiring a feature you builtNo published account of a named company removing an LLM from production could be found. The absence is taught in Chapter 2 as a finding; it is not enough to build a chapter on.
  • Splitting Chapter 7If the security material outgrows one chapter, the split is into what it may do and what you recorded, never into attacks and defences.
