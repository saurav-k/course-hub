The number did not move

6 stagesprint-friendly You made a change that should have helped and it did not. What to check, in order, cheapest first. Work down and stop at the first row that fires.

Stage 0 comes before every other stage and is skipped most often. Before you conclude that a technique failed, establish that your measurement could have seen it succeed. Roughly half the "it did not help" reports in this course's material are a measurement that was never capable of moving, and every one of those costs a day if you debug the technique instead.

Stage 0. Could the measurement have seen it?

CheckWhat you are looking forDeveloped at
Did you change exactly one artefact? Corpus, prompt, model id and eval set each move on their own schedule. Change two and a flat result has two candidate causes and no owner; the count of distinct systems is the product of the four. 0100
Is the change actually in the run? Clone into a fresh directory and re-run the recorded command. A change living in an uncommitted file, a shell variable or a warm cache is the commonest cause of a flat number. 1000
Is the move larger than your noise? Run the identical system five times and look at the spread. On a thirty-item set a score near 0.8 carries roughly ±7 points of standard error, so a three-point "improvement" is not one. 0660
Can your judge resolve a move this size? Any model-scored metric is bounded by the judge's agreement with your hand-scoring. If it agrees on 17 of 20, a move from 0.78 to 0.81 is the judge, not the system. 0170
Does the metric measure what you changed? Two of the five generation metrics score the retriever. Improving generation and watching context precision is watching the wrong number. The eval harness

Stage 1. Retrieval did not improve

SymptomCheckDeveloped at
Recall is flat however you tune the retriever. Was the answering passage ever a candidate? Read the retrieval span's document ids and scores. A passage at rank 700 of 13,000 is a vocabulary failure, not a ranking one, and no k fixes it. 0160, 0150
Recall collapsed after you added a filter. Filtering applied after the index scan returns far fewer rows than you asked for. This reads as "the model does not know about our EU documents" and is an index behaviour rather than a knowledge gap. 0140
Everything got worse after an embedding change. Changing the embedding model invalidates the whole index. If you did not re-embed the corpus, you are querying one model's space with another model's vectors. 0130
Your dense retriever does not beat keyword search. That is a published result rather than a mistake, and it is why the baseline is BM25. If it does not beat the baseline, it has not earned its cost. 0160
Recall is near 1.0 on your first try. The questions were probably written from the passages. Rewrite three the way a colleague who has not read the documents would ask, and measure again. 0160

Stage 2. The agent did not get better

SymptomCheckDeveloped at
The answer score is unchanged and the agent "feels" worse. An answer score is blind to the path. Compare tool trajectories under a named match rule; a right answer reached by four redundant calls scores the same as a good one. 0370
It worked when you tried it and fails for others. One trial is not a measurement. Run the same task k times and ask whether all k succeeded; single-trial and repeated-trial scores diverge sharply in published results. 0370
Adding a reflection pass changed nothing. Reflection helps when the second pass carries evidence the first did not have. A self-critique with no external signal is the case two papers disagree about, and the reconciliation is that the signal has to come from outside. 0360
Runs stop early and nobody reported an error. A ceiling is firing with no user-visible degradation behind it. Check the turn, token and deadline limits and what the caller sees when each one trips. 0310
The agent repeats a failing call instead of recovering. An error the model cannot see is an error it cannot correct. Domain errors belong in the tool result; only transport errors should be raised. 0330

Stage 3. Latency or cost did not move

SymptomCheckDeveloped at
You shipped streaming and the dashboard is unchanged. Expected. Streaming changes when the first token arrives, not the total. You need the separate time-to-first-chunk histogram to see it at all. 0620
You tuned retrieval and the p95 barely moved. Your own code is usually a few per cent of the request. Generation is rate times length, and output length is the only one of the three terms you control. 0620
The prompt cache never reports a hit. Something in the prefix moves per request. A timestamp, a request id, a user's name in the system prompt, or a set serialised in a different order each time - the last is the hardest to spot. 0630
Neither a write nor a read is reported. The prefix is probably below the model's minimum cacheable length, which varies by model. No error is returned when a marked prompt is too short. 0630
Cost and latency both fell and quality did not change. Be suspicious rather than pleased. Answers getting shorter, a silent model fallback, or a semantic cache serving near-misses all look like this. Check output tokens and finish reasons first. 0670, 0640

Stage 4. It works locally and not in production

SymptomCheckDeveloped at
Pods restart in a loop during a provider incident. A liveness probe is calling the model. With the shipped defaults that kills every pod about thirty seconds into an outage you could not have fixed, and they stay down after it clears. 0610
The fleet is saturated and the autoscaler does nothing. A worker waiting on a socket uses almost no CPU, so a resource-metric target never leaves its tolerance band. Scale on queue depth or in-flight requests instead. 0670
One call becomes many under load. Your retry loop is multiplying with the SDK's own automatic retries rather than replacing them. Set the client's maximum-retries option explicitly and keep policy at one level. 0610
The eval gate fails and passes on re-run. The threshold is inside the sampling band. Widen it, enlarge the set, or gate on the delta - but do not add a re-run button. 0660
Everything is green and users are complaining. Every failure mode in this course returns HTTP 200. Look at output tokens, finish reasons, cache hit share and the last nightly eval rather than at error rates. 0670

Stage 5. It moved, and you cannot say why

The opposite problem, and the more dangerous one, because an unattributed improvement is repeated by superstition.

CheckWhat to doDeveloped at
More than one artefact changed between the two runs. Revert to the baseline, apply the changes one at a time, and re-measure at each step. The regression you can attribute in a minute and the one you cannot differ only in whether somebody did this. 0100
The corpus moved under you. Documents somebody else maintains and re-exports on their own schedule are a data dependency. Pin and record the corpus version with every number. 0100
The number has no k, no set and no corpus version. Then it is not a number, and nothing can be concluded from its movement. Re-record it in the reporting form before drawing any inference. The eval harness
Two habits this sheet is written against. The first is debugging the technique before the measurement, which is why stage 0 exists and is ordered first. The second is treating a flat result as a failure of the idea. A change that produced nothing, recorded with its numbers, is a real finding - module 2 requires at least one such row, and the two module 6 lessons a reader may correctly finish by declining are the same principle. A capstone in which every idea worked is a capstone somebody faked.
