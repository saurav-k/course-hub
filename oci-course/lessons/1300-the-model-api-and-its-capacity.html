The model is hosted, and the interesting choice is whose capacity it runs on

working 10 min Capability area ai-ml, matrix row managed-model-api.

The one-minute version

  • The Generative AI service hosts foundation models from several providers, and which models are available varies by region.
  • Capacity comes two ways: shared, and dedicated AI clusters that give single-tenant capacity for production.
  • Fine-tuning is offered, using parameter-efficient approaches rather than retraining a whole model, and a path exists to bring a custom model in.
  • Oracle states that your data is not used by the providers to train their models, which is the sentence a review will ask about.
  • Content moderation is part of this service. There is no separate first-party screening product sitting in front of arbitrary models, so filtering is what the hosted service applies.

The mental model: three ways to get an answer from a model

The mechanism: tuning, and where the tuned model runs

The reframing Treat a hosted model as a regional service with a capacity model, not as an infinite interface. Which models exist depends on the region; how predictable your throughput is depends on whether the capacity is shared or yours; and both questions have to be answered before an application depends on the answer.

The mechanism: what the service includes, stated carefully

Two facts here are the kind that rot, and both carry their source for that reason. Which model families are offered, and in which regions, changes as the service develops, so the vendor page is the authority rather than any summary of it. Check both before a design commits to a particular model in a particular region, and remember from lesson 0101 that a tenancy has to be subscribed to the region at all.

The trade-off: dedicated capacity costs whether or not you use it

Shared capacity is the cheap way to start and the reason experiments are nearly free. Its throughput is not yours alone, so a production workload with a latency expectation is making an assumption it cannot enforce.

A dedicated cluster makes the capacity predictable and makes it a running cost that exists whether traffic arrives or not. That is the same trade as the persistent cluster in lesson 0602 and the idle state in lesson 0203, arriving in a third place, and it is decided the same way: by asking whether an idle hour is buying anything.

Practice about 15 minutes, with paper

Practice 1working

A team wants to add a summarisation feature to a customer-facing application. They expect steady traffic during business hours and almost none overnight, they want the summaries to follow their own house style, and their compliance group asks three questions: where does the data go, what stops the model producing something inappropriate, and which region does this run in.

Design the approach, and answer the compliance group's three questions using only what the vendor documentation states.

Hint

The house style requirement points at one mechanism, and that mechanism decides the capacity question for you. Answer the compliance questions in the platform's own terms rather than in general ones, and be careful about the second.

Show the worked solution
  1. The house style. Fine-tune a base model on examples of the style. That is what tuning is for, and it avoids trying to express the style in every prompt.
  2. What that decides. A tuned model needs capacity of its own, so a dedicated cluster follows from the tuning decision rather than being a separate choice.
  3. The traffic pattern. Business-hours traffic with an idle night means the dedicated cluster is paid for while nothing is happening. That is the trade to state explicitly to whoever approves it, alongside the alternative of prompting a shared model instead of tuning.
  4. Where the data goes. Oracle states that provider models are not trained on your data. That is the sentence to cite, and it is a vendor statement rather than an inference.
  5. What stops inappropriate output. Content moderation within the service. Say precisely that, because there is no separate screening product to point at and claiming one would be wrong.
  6. Which region. Whichever region offers the model, and which models are offered varies by region. Check the vendor page, and confirm the tenancy is subscribed to that region at all.

Sanity check. The capacity answer should follow from the tuning answer rather than being chosen independently, and the second compliance answer should name moderation inside the service without inventing a product. If the design ended on shared capacity with a tuned model, ask where that tuned model is served from.

Your teacher. Ask an agent which regions your tenancy is subscribed to and which of them offer the model you intend to use. The intersection of those two lists is the real deployment surface, and it is often smaller than either list alone suggests.

Primary source to go deeper

Read the Generative AI documentation. It carries the model list, the regional availability, the tuning options and the dedicated cluster model, and it is the page to check rather than to remember, because all four change.
