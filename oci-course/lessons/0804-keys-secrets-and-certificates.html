Custody is a ladder with four rungs, and the top rung is still inside this cloud

working 11 min Capability area secrets-keys, matrix rows key-management, hsm, secrets-store and certificate-manager.

The one-minute version

  • Vault holds master encryption keys, software-protected or hardware-protected, and services use them through envelope encryption rather than encrypting data with them directly.
  • Custody is a ladder: a shared vault partition, a private vault with its own partition, and Dedicated Key Management where you administer the hardware partition and reach it directly.
  • The dedicated option needs a minimum of three partitions per cluster, which are kept in step with each other.
  • Secret Management is a separate service for versioned secrets with rotation schedules, and other services read registry credentials from it.
  • The Certificates service is both a managed internal authority with automatic renewal and a store for certificates issued elsewhere.

The mental model: the master key never touches the data

The mechanism: the four rungs of custody

The dedicated option is billed per partition-hour with a minimum of three partitions per cluster, kept synchronised with each other, so the floor is three partitions for every hour the cluster exists. That minimum is the thing to notice before it is the price: this is a cluster, not a key.

The mechanism: bringing your own key material

The reframing Ask who can administer the partition rather than who owns the key. Ownership is the same at every rung; what changes is whether Oracle or you hold the administrative role over the hardware, and whether the partition is shared. That question has a clear answer per rung, and "we own our keys" does not.

The mechanism: two kinds of certificate, one service

Secret Management is the third service here: versioned secrets with rotation schedules, one hundred and fifty of them free, and it is where Container Instances and Functions read the registry credentials of lesson 0302. It was separated from the Vault branding, so older material files it under a different name.

Two limits are worth carrying. API Gateway consumes certificate authority bundles with a default of two per gateway, which constrains designs that expect to trust many internal authorities at one gateway. And key versions are counted and billed beyond the free allowance, so a rotation schedule set aggressively across many keys is a cost as well as a hygiene decision.

The trade-off: exclusive custody costs a cluster

Moving up the ladder buys isolation and, at the top, administrative control of the partition and a direct interface to it. That is what a regulator or an internal control usually wants to see, and it is available without leaving the platform's own tooling.

What it costs is that the top rung is not a key any more, it is infrastructure: three synchronised partitions billed by the hour whether or not a key is being used, with partition users to administer. The rung below gives a partition of your own with Oracle administering the hardware, and for most designs that is where the honest answer sits.

Check yourself

Retrieval practice

Answer from memory before revealing.

What does a master encryption key in the vault actually encrypt?
Envelope encryption means the master key wraps a data key. The first is the model people assume, and it would make rotation ruinously expensive. The third and fourth attach the key to mechanisms from other pages entirely.
What does Dedicated Key Management give that a private vault does not?
The dedicated option hands you the partition users and a direct interface. The first is the pattern this ladder does not reach, and saying otherwise is the most consequential error on the page. The second is already true of a private vault. The third describes a feature of the key service rather than the difference.
Which certificates does the service renew for you automatically?
Automatic renewal applies to certificates the internal authority issued. The second inverts it. The third invents a schedule that would let the service renew a certificate it cannot issue. The fourth denies a documented feature outright.

Practice about 15 minutes, with paper

Practice 1working

An auditor asks four questions about a workload. Where are the keys that protect its stored data, and who can administer the hardware holding them. What proves the public web endpoint's identity, and who renews it. Where does the application's third-party API credential live. And what would happen if the master key were deleted.

Answer all four in the platform's own vocabulary, and for the first one name the rung of the ladder you would recommend and why.

Hint

Three of the four questions each name a different service on this page. The fourth is about envelope encryption and has a one-sentence answer that follows from what the master key actually wraps.

Show the worked solution
  1. The keys. Master encryption keys in a Vault. For most designs the recommendation is a private vault: a partition of your own, with Oracle administering the hardware. Move to Dedicated Key Management only if a control specifically requires that you administer the partition, because that rung is a three-partition cluster billed by the hour.
  2. The public endpoint. A certificate held in the Certificates service, obtained from an outside authority and imported, because the managed authority here is internal. Renewal is therefore yours to arrange rather than automatic.
  3. The third-party credential. Secret Management, as a versioned secret with a rotation schedule, read by the workload at run time rather than baked into an image.
  4. Deleting the master key. Every data key wrapped by it becomes unopenable, so the data is unrecoverable. This is the asymmetry of envelope encryption: rotation is cheap and deletion is final.
  5. What the auditor should also be told. At every rung of the ladder the hardware sits inside this cloud, so if their control requires a key held outside the provider, no option on this page satisfies it.

Sanity check. Three different services should appear in your first three answers. If the public certificate came out as automatically renewed, re-read which authority the automatic renewal belongs to.

Your teacher. Ask an agent which resources point at each master key in a vault. The answer is the true blast radius of that key, and it is the only honest input to a conversation about deleting or rotating one.
