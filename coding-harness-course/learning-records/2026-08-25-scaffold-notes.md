# Hue verification - coding-harness-course, --course-hue: 150

Date: 2026-08-25. Verifier: the scaffolding agent, per `new-course.md` step 3 registration 1.

## What was checked

The candidate hue is 150, the next free step on the 25-degree grid (occupied: -75, -50, -25, 0, 25, 50, 75, 100, 125).

1. **Chroma clipping.** The OKLCH rotation `oklch(from var(--accent) l c calc(h + 150))` can land outside sRGB at some hues, and the browser then clips chroma silently. Measured by rendering a swatch of the rotated accent next to the unrotated accent for all six palettes in both modes, reading painted pixels back through a canvas, and comparing chroma in OKLab space. Result: recorded in `learning-records/hue-check.json`, generated by `learning-records/hue-check.html`.
2. **Legibility across six palettes x two modes.** The same harness page renders the accent in chrome (spine wordmark, progress bars, rail chip) and in pills; each combination was looked at, not counted.

## Acceptance

The skill's bar: the seven original hues lose between 0 and 8 percent of their chroma on average and up to 55 percent in their worst cell, so a candidate inside that range is no worse than what is already published.
