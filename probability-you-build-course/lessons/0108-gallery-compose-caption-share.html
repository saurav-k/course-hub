The gallery: compose the piece, caption it honestly, prove it was sampled

10 min working Export a PNG, copy a link, and hand a stranger two artefacts that reconstruct your artwork exactly on their machine.

The one-minute version

  • All five layers compose into one piece; every layer keeps its own seeded stream, so toggling never disturbs its neighbours.
  • The caption under the build is generated from the live parameters: it is the artwork's specification, not a title.
  • PNG export and the parameter permalink turn "look what I made" into "here is exactly what I made" - reproducibility as a feature.
  • The week's controls each bought a lesson: seed, n, sigma, k, lambda, u, N steps, correlation. None was decoration.

1. Compose without entangling

Composition has one engineering rule this week: layers must not disturb each other. Every layer draws from its own seeded stream derived from the master seed, which buys a property you can test right now - toggle any layer off and back on, and every other layer holds its pixels exactly. That isolation is what makes the piece inspectable: you can interrogate one distribution while the others hold still, the way page 0101 asked you to interrogate the starfield alone.

2. Drive the full console

Note what the console does not offer: a colour-scheme slider, a sparkle-density knob, anything purely cosmetic. That absence is policy. Every control shipped this week moves a mathematical quantity and its visible consequence together, and a decoration dial would quietly teach the opposite lesson - that the knobs are aesthetic preferences. If you extend this piece for fun and add a hue slider, label it honestly in the caption as the one setting that carries no information.

3. The caption is the assessment

Here is the week's exit check, and it runs on the honour system: compose a piece you like, then read its generated caption aloud to someone and ask what they would expect Regenerate to change. If the sentence "keeps 2,600 proposals under exp(-3r), acceptance 14.0%" produces the answer "the nebula's size and brightness, together", the piece has transmitted its own mathematics. Each phrase maps to one layer, one distribution, one parameter you can defend - because you watched each one earn its effect on pages 0103 through 0107.

That table, plus the seed, is a complete public description of the artwork. Two people who never meet, given the table and the seed, will produce byte-identical canvases - and the moment either deviates from the recipe, the pictures diverge visibly. Reproducibility is not a convenience bolted onto this course; it is how a probabilistic claim gets audited, which is precisely where Week 6 will take it.

Check yourself

Why does the caption name distributions and parameters instead of moods?
The named distributions plus the seed are exactly sufficient to regenerate the work anywhere - which makes claims checkable and taste arguable in public. Style points are beside the point, file formats do not demand it, and "technical reads serious" is the exact inversion of this course's ethic: the nouns are there because they do work.
A stranger opens your permalink on their own machine. What do they get?
Same seed, same samplers, same order - the function returns its one value, pixel for pixel, which is the whole doctrine of page 0101 made useful. A style match would mean the seed leaked away somewhere; needing the PNG would concede the recipe failed; and "approximately" is precisely the word reproducibility exists to delete.
Your teacher. Ship the thing. Export one PNG, copy one permalink, and send both to somebody who does not care about probability, along with the caption. If they come back having regenerated your exact piece on their phone, you have closed the loop PAI1 promises its students - a public portfolio artefact that survives a sceptical reviewer.
