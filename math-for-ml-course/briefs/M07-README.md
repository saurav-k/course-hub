# M07 Probability - brief register

Fifteen briefs, covering the fourteen planned lessons of issue #50 plus one split.

**Page numbers here are PLACEHOLDERS.** `M07-01` to `M07-15` are the teaching order within the
module. The real `NNNN` numbers are assigned when the scaffold (#41) lands and the module block is
known; every brief is then renamed to `NNNN-<slug>.md` and its prerequisites are rewritten from
placeholder to number. Nothing outside this folder carries a placeholder.

## Reconciliation with issue #50

Issue #50 lists fourteen lessons. This module ships fifteen. Three deliberate differences, all
additive:

| #50 lesson | Here | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1. The axioms, and what a probability is a probability of | **M07-01** and **M07-02** | Two tight ideas. What a probability *means* is not the same page as the three axioms it obeys, and the house rule is one idea per page. |
| 9. Bernoulli and Binomial | **M07-10**, which also carries the categorical | One family at three widths is one idea, and the softmax head is a categorical. Leaving the categorical out would leave the course's most-used distribution untaught. |
| 10. Poisson, 13. Exponential and Geometric | **M07-12** geometric, **M07-13** Poisson **and** exponential together | Poisson and exponential are one process read two ways, which is the idea that makes both memorable. The geometric is its own page because memorylessness in discrete time is where it is learned. |

Order also differs from #50: the distribution pages run discrete family, uniform, waiting,
counting-and-gaps, normal. That puts every discrete distribution before every continuous one, so
`M05`'s integrals are needed only from **M07-11** onward.

## The module dataset

`datasets/requests.csv`, 25,000 rows, 1.3 MB, generated by `datasets/generate/make_requests.py`.
An inference-endpoint request log. It exists because neither shared dataset has arrival times, counts
in a window, or a class rare enough for a base rate to bite, and this module needs all three.

Measured properties, all designed in, all quoted on the pages that use them:

| Column | Property | Serves |
|---|---|---|
| `arrival_s` | Poisson process at 2.3/s. Gaps: mean 0.43538 s, rate-hat 2.2969. `P(gap > 1s)` = 0.09928 against `e^-2.3` = 0.10026. Memorylessness holds directly: `P(gap > 1.5 | gap > 0.5)` = 0.10073. | M07-13 |
| per-second counts | 10,884 windows, mean 2.2967, variance 2.3388. Empirical PMF matches Poisson to within 0.005 at every k from 0 to 8. `P(N >= 5)` = 0.0858 against 0.0834. | M07-13 |
| `retries` | Geometric(0.85), support starts at **1**. Mean 1.1786 against `1/p` = 1.1765. The other convention would predict 0.176, off by exactly one. | M07-12 |
| `route` | Categorical, deliberately unequal: chat 0.5972, embed 0.3032, rerank 0.0996. | M07-10 |
| `cache_hit` vs `verified_user` | **Independent by construction.** `P(both)` = 0.174440 against the product 0.174685, a gap of 0.000245. | M07-05 |
| `flagged` vs `verified_user` | **Dependent by construction.** `P(both)` = 0.000760 against the product 0.002775. `P(flagged | unverified)` = 0.010694 and `P(flagged | verified)` = 0.001085, a ratio of 9.86. | M07-04, M07-05 |
| `flagged` | Rare: 99 of 25,000, a rate of 0.003960. Always-predict-clean scores 99.604% accuracy. | M07-07 |
| `abuse_score` | Overlapping class scores, so every threshold trades. At 0.5: recall 0.9192, FPR 0.0116, **precision 0.2395**. At 0.6: recall 0.7374, precision 0.6033. | M07-06, M07-07 |
| `latency_ms` | 97% Normal(180, 28) with a heavy 3% tail. Whole column: 79.58% within 1 sd against 68.27% predicted, and 0.908% above +3 sd against 0.135% predicted, **6.7 times too many**. Bulk under 300 ms: 69.49 / 95.45 / 99.44, almost exactly normal. | M07-14 |

`sessions.csv` is reused where it fits rather than adding a second module dataset.
