What this is, and what it is not

It is a map from a question you might be asked to the page that answers it. It is not a shorter course. Every entry points at a page that develops the idea properly, because an interview answer assembled from a bullet list falls over on the first follow-up question.

The gap this index covers is wider than it looks. The mathematics an interview reaches for is mostly linear algebra, probability and estimation, and those are the parts a general data science syllabus tends to spend least time on.
