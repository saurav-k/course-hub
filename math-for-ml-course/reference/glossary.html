Glossary

reference grows with the course One sentence per term, grouped by idea rather than alphabetically, and linked to the page that develops it.

This sheet is a skeleton and it grows with the course. A term is added here on the day the page that introduces it lands, never before. An entry linking a page that does not exist yet fails the validator, so entries arrive with their pages.

How an entry is written

One sentence, in the words a reader would use rather than the words a textbook would. The sentence names what the thing is, not what it is for, because the use belongs on the page and the identity belongs here. Where a term has a second meaning elsewhere in machine learning, the entry says so: a kernel in this course is not a CUDA kernel, and that collision has already cost one reader an afternoon.
