The road ahead

not yet written

The eleven modules, in the order they are taught, which is also the order their file numbers run in: reading lessons/ in ascending order is always legal, because no page needs a page that comes after it. Only Lesson 00 exists on this site so far, so the entries below are a roadmap rather than links. The eleven modules name 118 pages between them: 116 are still roadmap entries below, and the rest are written and linked above. The finished course is expected to run to about 133 pages, because a page that carries a named theorem owes its stated proof and some of these will not fit that under the word ceiling. A page over the ceiling is two pages, and splitting beats cutting.

Two orderings are deliberate and worth knowing. Module 04 comes before calculus, because everything in it is calculus-free once PCA is derived through the SVD. Module 02 comes second, because summarising data needs nothing but Module 01, and a reader should meet a histogram long before they meet the Central Limit Theorem. A reader who wants only probability and statistics can read Module 01, then 02, then 07 to 09, taking Module 05's two pages on integrals first.

How this course reads

what to expect
Every page
A picture before the formula
No formula appears before a figure showing what it means, and every symbol is named in words on the page that uses it. At least one figure per page is a hand-drawn chart, because no diagram library can draw a distribution or a vector.
Every theorem
A stated proof, not an assertion
Where the course names a theorem it states the proof, written to be read: the shape of the argument and the one step that does the real work. Where a full proof is beyond the course, the page says so and proves the case the course needs.
Every result
A program you can run
Self-contained NumPy and Pandas, against a generated dataset big enough that hand calculation is impossible. It computes the answer once from the definition and once the library way, and asserts they agree. The hand-worked example stays.
Every page
Problems you attempt first
Two quizzes and at least one multi-step problem, with a hint before the solution and a sanity check after it, so you can catch your own arithmetic rather than only discover that your number differed.
