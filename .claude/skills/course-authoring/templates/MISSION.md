# Mission

The record of the interview in `.claude/skills/course-authoring/new-course.md`.
This file is canonical: when a later authoring decision is argued, it is settled by re-reading this, not by re-deciding.

## Why this course exists

Who the learner is, in one sentence naming their job and their years in it.
What they already know that this course may assume without teaching.
The specific cold spot: the thing they once knew, never knew, or know as folklore.

Name the gap precisely.
"New to X" and "knew X and it has gone cold" produce different courses, and getting this wrong makes every page slightly wrong.

## The source

The spine this course follows, if it follows one: a deck, a syllabus, a paper series, a specification.
Where a spine exists, its numbers are the ceiling and its ordering is the default.

Delete this section if the course has no spine, and say in `RESOURCES.md` what the canon is instead.

## Success looks like

The learner can:

- Do a thing, phrased as an action rather than a topic.
- Do a second thing.
- Do a third, a fourth, a fifth.

And the failure that would still be a failure even if every page were accurate.

## Structure

The page shape: lesson, chapter, or lecture split into parts. One shape, held for the whole course.
How many pages, how many modules, and what one page costs in prose words.

## The ladder

The rungs, and what a learner arriving at each one already has.

- **Foundation** (`pill easy`): arrives cold. Every term is defined here or in a named earlier page.
- **Working** (`pill med`): has the foundation pages. Can be given a mechanism and a trade-off directly.
- **Frontier** (`pill hard`): has the working pages. Can be handed an open question or a live disagreement.

Which pages sit at which rung.
If this course has no genuine progression, say so plainly here and call it a reference work rather than claim a ladder the pages do not have.

## Constraints

The rules specific to this course that the house standard does not already carry.
Keep them few and keep them real: a constraint that restates the house standard is noise, and a constraint nobody could violate is not a constraint.

## Out of scope

What this course does not cover, and for each, the neighbour that owns it.
"Out of scope" with nowhere to send the reader is an abandonment.

## Siblings

Which courses in this hub this one touches, at which pages, and what each adds that this one does not.

## Revisit when

The event that should make someone reopen this file: a module finished, a source updated, a shape that turned out wrong.
