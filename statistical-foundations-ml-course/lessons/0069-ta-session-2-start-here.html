  • This session drills what Lecture 4 and Lecture 5 built: independence as a product test, inclusion-exclusion, conditional probability, and Bayes.

Where this sits

This is the second tutorial session of the course and it lands directly after Lecture 5. Where TA Session 1 audited the axioms and the definitions, this one audits the two things the lectures have just finished building: whether events are independent, and how a probability moves when you learn something.

How this session is arranged

The session ran over two days, Saturday and Sunday, with a separate set of scribe notes for each. The two sets overlap: the entire conditional-probability and Bayes theory block appears, word for word, in both. Rather than teach it twice, it is taught once on Part 3 and the overlap is named there.

Every question is worked to the same fixed shape TA Session 1 established, because a tutorial read alone is a bad teacher: the questions are terse and the official notes compress three lines of algebra into one.

  1. The question restated in full, so you never hold it in your head while reading the answer.
  2. Every symbol named in words, on the page where it appears.
  3. The governing theorem named, and justified: why this tool and not another.
  4. The picture before the algebra. A grid for the coin space, Venn circles for the divisibility counts, a tree for every Bayes question.
  5. Every intermediate step, including the ones the notes skip.
  6. The answer stated plainly, with the source's own mark allocation kept where it gives one.
  7. Where this goes wrong: the specific trap the question was built around.
  8. A check you can run on the answer without redoing the work.

Two honest notes before you start

The two days overlap, and one of them is a duplicateThe Saturday and Sunday scribe notes carry the conditional-probability and Bayes theory block identically: same statements, same figure, same worked identity. This course teaches it once, on Part 3, and says there that both days gave it. Nothing is lost, and nothing that appeared in only one of the two days has been dropped.
The question numbers start partway through, and stay that wayThe source numbers its questions 3, 10, 25 and 30, which are positions in a larger problem sheet rather than a sequence of their own, and two of the Sunday questions carry no number at all. Those numbers are kept exactly as the notes give them and are never renumbered to run from one. Where a question has no number, this course says so rather than inventing one.
