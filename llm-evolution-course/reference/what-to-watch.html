Frontier watch

The questions this course cannot answer yet, each written so that a future reader can tell whether it has been answered. The chronicle records what happened. This page records what has not happened yet and what would count as it happening.

Moving Accurate as of 22 August 2026. Next review due February 2027. Entries are appended, never edited. The only thing that changes on a published entry is its status word.

Last reviewed: 2026-08-22. This date moves on every review pass, including a pass that adds nothing. A living page with no review date cannot be told apart from an abandoned one.

This page is deliberately less reliable than the lessons. A lesson is written after the argument about it has finished, and it is edited only when the mechanism underneath it changes. An entry here is written while the argument is still running, from whatever primary source existed on the day it was added, and it is never rewritten afterwards even when it turns out to be wrong. Read a lesson as the course's considered position. Read an entry here as a dated bet with its evidence attached, and check the date before you rely on it.

The append-only contract

Five rules. They are what make a page that changes safe to cite.

  1. An entry is never edited after it is published. Its claim, its date and its source stay exactly as first written, even when the claim turns out to be wrong.
  2. The only permitted mutation of an existing entry is its data-status attribute and the matching pill text, which moves from watching to confirmed, faded or promoted. One word, and nothing else on the entry.
  3. A correction is an appended dated paragraph inside the entry, written as <p class="update">, with the original sentence left standing above it. A history course that quietly rewrites its own claims is teaching the opposite of what it says.
  4. Anchors are permanent. Every entry carries id="YYYY-MM-slug" and that anchor is a public URL like any lesson. Add anchors freely, change them never.
  5. Entries are appended at the bottom and displayed in the order they were added. Reversing them visually would put the reading order of the page out of step with its source order, which breaks tab order and the order a screen reader announces. Recency is served by the link above instead.

The promotion rule

This is what stops the page becoming a graveyard of links.

An entry with status confirmed that has survived two review cycles and carries a primary source is promoted into a lesson, or into a dated paragraph appended to the frontier snapshot in Where we are now. On promotion the entry's status becomes promoted and a link to the lesson is appended to it. The entry itself is never deleted.

The address is chosen when the entry is added, not when it is promoted, and it is the third pill on every entry below. Writing it down at the start is what turns this page from a link dump into a backlog for the course.

Entries

Does test-time compute keep paying, or does it saturate?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0049

The claim. As of 22 August 2026 the published evidence is two-sided: independent work measures diminishing returns at high thinking budgets, and cases where extended reasoning makes a model abandon an answer it had already got right, while no frontier laboratory has published a curve that flattens on an unsaturated benchmark.

Why it matters. The capability curve from 2024 onwards rests on spending more computation per question, which is the axis Thinking is just more tokens is about. A published plateau from a laboratory, or a frontier release whose headline gain comes from a third axis, would settle it.

Source: When More Thinking Hurts: Overthinking in LLM Test-Time Compute Scaling, arXiv 2604.10739, read 2026-08-21, with the saturation archetypes in arXiv 2505.20522.

Can a model learn continually, or is the context window all the memory there is?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0048

The claim. As of 22 August 2026 no deployed frontier model updates its own weights from what happens after training, so everything a system appears to learn in service lives in a context window or an external store and the old stability-and-plasticity problem simply reappears at the retrieval layer.

Why it matters. An agent that cannot absorb what it did yesterday cannot compound, which caps long-horizon autonomy however good the reasoning is, and it is the reason Long context: capacity is not competence separates holding text from using it. The tripwire is a shipped model that updates weights from deployment and documents how it avoids forgetting.

Source: When Continual Learning Moves to Memory, arXiv 2604.27003, read 2026-08-21.

Is hallucination an engineering problem or a structural one?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0038

The claim. The September 2025 argument that hallucination is an incentive artefact, because mainstream benchmarks score a confident guess above an admission of ignorance, had not produced a headline benchmark rewarding calibrated abstention at any major laboratory as of 22 August 2026.

Why it matters. It says the fix is in how the field grades, not in the architecture, which is exactly the kind of claim Reading an eval table like a scientist teaches a reader to test. Published rates are reported per benchmark and are not comparable across benchmarks, so no single number belongs on this page.

Source: Kalai, Nachum, Vempala and Zhang, Why Language Models Hallucinate, arXiv 2509.04664, read 2026-08-21.

Do world models become the next substrate, or stay a research demo?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0055

The claim. Genie 3, announced on 5 August 2025 as a real-time interactive world model running at 24 frames per second and 720p with a few minutes of physical consistency, was still the only model of its kind from a frontier laboratory as of 22 August 2026.

Why it matters. If the next jump comes from simulating an environment rather than predicting text, the language model becomes a component rather than the platform, which is the boundary Multimodality, and the model as an interface is drawn along. A second laboratory shipping one would make it a category rather than one company's project.

Source: Google DeepMind, Genie 3: A new frontier for world models, read 2026-08-21.

Does the open-weight gap close, hold, or reopen?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0040

The claim. The weights got more open and the licences got less open at the same time: Kimi K3, the largest open-weight release to date at 2.8 trillion parameters with 104 billion active, ships under its own licence requiring a separate agreement above twenty million US dollars of service revenue, and Moonshot's own card says open weight rather than open source.

Why it matters. Four axes of open exists because "open" is four separate questions, and this is the axis moving backwards while the others move forwards. The tripwire in either direction is the next flagship release: a plain permissive licence, or another revenue threshold.

Source: Kimi K3 model card, Hugging Face, read 2026-08-22.

Does compute bind first, or does power?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0056

The claim. Epoch AI's frontier data-centre tracking expects five separate facilities above one gigawatt, each from a different operator, to come online during 2026, and no frontier training run had been publicly delayed or relocated for power reasons as of 22 August 2026.

Why it matters. Whichever of the two binds sets the ceiling on capability growth, and they have completely different fixes on completely different timescales, since chips are fabricated in quarters and grid connections take years. It is the contested half of Where we are now, and one named delayed training run would settle it.

Source: Epoch AI, Frontier Data Centers Hub, read 2026-08-22. A named method with published data rather than an aggregator, which is why it is cited here.

Does AI regulation converge, or keep fragmenting?

added 2026-08-22 watching would open a new lesson

The claim. Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, in force on 27 July 2026, moved the AI Act's Annex III high-risk obligations to 2 December 2027 and its Annex I embedded high-risk obligations to 2 August 2028, without deferring the general application date.

Why it matters. This course carries no regulatory lesson at all, deliberately, because the timetable has moved twice while the course was being written and a lesson would have been wrong on the day it shipped. The entry holds the fact until the dates stop moving, so a future lesson does not have to rediscover it.

Source: Regulation (EU) 2026/1744, EUR-Lex, read 2026-08-22.

Do agents become reliable enough for unattended long-horizon work?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0054

The claim. METR's Time Horizon 1.1, published on 29 January 2026, puts the doubling time of the task length an agent completes at fifty per cent reliability at 196 days across the full trend and 89 days since 2024, and no laboratory has published the equivalent curve at a reliability anyone would deploy against.

Why it matters. Fifty per cent reliability is not a deployable reliability, and the distance from there to ninety-nine per cent on the same task length is the whole engineering problem that Why agents are a systems problem describes. A published high-reliability horizon that is also growing would answer it.

Source: METR, Time Horizon 1.1, read 2026-08-21.

Can Chinese laboratories keep leading open weights under export controls?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0008

The claim. DeepSeek had still not shipped R2 as of 22 August 2026, a year after the Financial Times reported, citing three people familiar with the matter, that a Huawei Ascend training run never completed and the company reverted to Nvidia for training while keeping Ascend for inference.

Why it matters. The open-weight ecosystem the world now downloads is largely Chinese, and The open-weights turn is the lesson that would need revisiting if the compute constraint started to bite. The tripwire is a Chinese frontier model trained end to end on domestic accelerators and confirmed as such by the laboratory.

Source: Reuters, reporting the Financial Times, 14 August 2025, read 2026-08-22. Reporting rather than a document either company published, and attributed here for that reason.

Has Mistral's reported round actually closed?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0056

The claim. Every sighting of Mistral's 2026 raise is a report of talks rather than a closing, so as of 22 August 2026 the company's last confirmed equity round remains the 1.7 billion euro Series C at an 11.7 billion euro valuation in September 2025.

Why it matters. Valuations are the most-repeated and least-checked numbers in this field, and the discipline this course teaches is that a reported negotiation is not a completed transaction. Nothing goes into Where we are now until the company itself announces a closing.

Source: Bloomberg, Mistral in funding talks at about 20 billion valuation, 12 June 2026, read 2026-08-22. Reporting of talks, and cited here as the reason for not printing a figure.

Have the Muse Spark 1.2 open weights shipped?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0041

The claim. Meta published the 30-billion-parameter Muse Glimmer under Apache 2.0 on 10 August 2026 and committed the same day to opening the weights of the flagship Muse Spark 1.2 in the coming weeks, and those weights had not shipped as of 22 August 2026.

Why it matters. Who ships open weights now is a dated measurement, and the difference between a shipped artefact and a stated intention is exactly what that page counts. Do not read Glimmer's licence as Spark's until the Spark weights exist.

Source: Meta, Introducing Muse Spark, read 2026-08-22, with the Glimmer release and the same-day commitment reported by CNBC, 10 August 2026.

Which model holds the current time-horizon record?

added 2026-08-22 watching would land in 0054

The claim. No figure for the current record is written down here on purpose: METR corrected its own model in March 2026, lowering recent fifty-per-cent horizon estimates by ten to twenty per cent, and its own task suite makes measurements above sixteen hours unreliable, so the live page is the citation and a frozen number is not.

Why it matters. Why agents are a systems problem quotes METR with a date attached for this reason. A laboratory that publishes a correction to its own headline result, with a sensitivity analysis, is a better model of source discipline than any benchmark number it produced.

Source: METR, time horizons, read 2026-08-21.
