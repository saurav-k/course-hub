Chronicle

The append-only record of what happened after a lesson was written. Adding a new development to this course costs one entry here and no edit to any lesson, which is the mechanism that lets the lessons stay stable while the field does not.

Moving Accurate as of 21 August 2026. Next review due February 2027. Entries are never edited and never deleted. A wrong entry is corrected by a later entry that links back to it.

Why this page exists

A lesson narrates a mechanism. This page narrates events. Keeping the two apart is what makes the course cheap to maintain: a lesson about how a router works does not need editing when another model ships, only when routing itself changes. Everything that is news rather than mechanism belongs here.

The three zones

Every page in this course carries a zone badge and an as-of date. The zone decides how it may be edited.

  • Settled Mechanisms and events whose interpretation is stable. Corrected by appending, never rewritten to accommodate a new event.
  • Moving Roughly the last eighteen months. Rewritten freely and explicitly provisional. Carries a review date.
  • Open Named questions with no answer. A question is retired with its answer and the date, never silently deleted.

The boundary between settled and moving is one date, and moving it is a deliberate reviewed act rather than a drift. The current horizon is 1 March 2025. BUILDER-SPEC.md holds the promotion ritual.

How to append an entry

Copy the block below, put it at the top of the entries list, fill it in, and change nothing else on this page. Every claim carries one of the four labels, and every label that is not KNOWN carries a link to whatever evidence does exist.

<article class="chronicle-entry" id="YYYY-MM-DD-short-slug" data-date="YYYY-MM-DD">
  <h3>What happened</h3>
  <p class="entry-meta">
    <time datetime="YYYY-MM-DD">1 January 2027</time>
    <span class="claim claim-known">KNOWN</span>
    <a href="https://example.org/primary-source">Primary source</a>
    <span class="affects">Affects: <a href="../lessons/0056-where-we-are-now.html">Where we are now</a></span>
  </p>
  <p>Two or three sentences. What happened, and which lesson it bears on.</p>
</article>

The four labels: KNOWN stated in a document you can open, INFERRED derived from evidence with the derivation shown, MARKETING published by the seller and not independently checked, UNVERIFIABLE no public evidence exists.
