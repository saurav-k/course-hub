Sparsity moves to attention

Once the feed-forward half of the block is cheap, attention is what is left paying the quadratic bill, so the same selective trick was pointed at attention itself.

Mixture of experts, part one: the router and Mixture of experts, part two: why it is hard make the feed-forward half of the block sparse. This lesson is about where the cost went next. It assumes you know both that attention grows with the square of the length and what a router does.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • Mixture of experts, part two: why it is hard A router that is trained only to be useful will send almost everything to a few experts, so every practical system needs something that stops the router eating itself, and three generations of that fix are the real story.
  • Serving reality: the cache, and what it costs Generating a token requires re-reading everything the model has already seen, so serving is a memory problem before it is an arithmetic problem, and most of the engineering since 2022 is about that memory.

The one tight idea

Once the feed-forward half of the block is cheap, attention is what is left paying the quadratic bill, so the same selective trick was pointed at attention itself.
