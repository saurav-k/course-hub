The Transformer: what dropping recurrence unlocked

Delete the recurrence and keep only attention, and every position in a sentence can be computed at the same time with a constant number of operations between any two words.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • Attention: stop compressing Do not squeeze the source sentence into one vector at all: keep every position, and at each output step learn a weighting that says which positions matter now.
  • The other problem: attention did not make it fast Attention fixed how far a model could reach, but the recurrence underneath it still forced one step per word, so training time grew with sentence length no matter how many machines you had.

The one tight idea

Delete the recurrence and keep only attention, and every position in a sentence can be computed at the same time with a constant number of operations between any two words.

2. What this lesson must cover

To write. Full prose, complete sentences, 1,400 to 2,000 words. Every bullet below is a beat the finished lesson owes the reader, in this order.

  • The architecture at a glance: a stack of blocks, each with self-attention and a small feed-forward network, with residual connections and normalisation.
  • Self-attention as the same mechanism from lesson 20 pointed at the sentence itself.
  • Multiple heads in one paragraph: several attentions in parallel, each free to look for something different.
  • Position: attention is order-blind, so order must be added, and the elegant sinusoidal answer was not the one that won in the end.
  • The cost that was accepted: work grows with the square of the sequence length.
  • That it was a translation paper with no claim to generality.
Do not overclaim

Do not derive the scaled dot product here. One intuition-level formula at most, then hand off to the papers course. This course owns why it mattered, not how to implement it.
