When meaning became geometry

Neural networks turned words from symbols that are either identical or unrelated into positions in a space where closeness means similarity, and that single change is what made everything after it possible.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • Before the machine could read For fifty years the only two ways to make a machine handle language were to write the rules by hand or to count how often words follow each other, and counting won.

The one tight idea

Neural networks turned words from symbols that are either identical or unrelated into positions in a space where closeness means similarity, and that single change is what made everything after it possible.

4. Where this hands off

This course owns chronology, causation, people and dates. It hands off for mechanism, maths and code rather than re-deriving them.

  • Deep dive: What a neural network actually is A network is a stack of two operations, multiply by a table of numbers and then bend the result, and training is nudging those numbers until a measured error gets smaller.
  • Deep dive: Backpropagation, and the two winters The field stalled twice for two different reasons: the first was an argument about what a single layer could represent, and the second was an obstacle in the arithmetic of training deep stacks.
  • Deep dive: Sequences, memory, and the LSTM A recurrent network is one small network applied again and again with a carried-over memory, and the LSTM is that memory given a protected lane and gates that decide what enters it.
  • Deep dive: 2012: the year the hardware caught up The ideas that won in 2012 were roughly twenty-five years old, and what actually changed was a large labelled dataset and a graphics card that could do the arithmetic in parallel.
  • Deep dive: Meaning as a direction in space Give every word a position in a few hundred dimensions, learn those positions only from the company each word keeps, and similarity in meaning becomes closeness in space.
