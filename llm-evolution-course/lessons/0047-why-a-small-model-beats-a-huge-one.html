Why a small model now beats a huge one from 2020

A model a fraction of the size now outperforms the largest models of 2020 on most tasks, and almost none of the reason is a better architecture.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • Chinchilla, the correction, and data as the new constraint The first scaling recipe held the wrong thing fixed, so the models built on it were far larger than they needed to be and far less trained than they should have been, and once that was corrected the scarce resource became text.
  • Teaching a model to answer A model trained to continue text is not trying to be helpful, so you add a stage whose only job is preference: show it demonstrations, learn a stand-in for human taste, and let it practise against that on a short leash.

The one tight idea

A model a fraction of the size now outperforms the largest models of 2020 on most tasks, and almost none of the reason is a better architecture.
