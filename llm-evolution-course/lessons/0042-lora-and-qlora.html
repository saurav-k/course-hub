LoRA and QLoRA: fine-tuning for the rest of us

You can adapt a large model by training a small number of extra numbers alongside the frozen originals, and if you also store the originals at lower precision the whole job fits on one ordinary machine.

Settled Accurate as of 21 August 2026. Interpretation is stable. Corrected by appending, never rewritten to fit a new event.
Where this lesson starts

The open-weights ecosystem needed a way to specialise a model without a data centre. This lesson is that way. It assumes you know what training a weight means, from What a neural network actually is.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • What a neural network actually is A network is a stack of two operations, multiply by a table of numbers and then bend the result, and training is nudging those numbers until a measured error gets smaller.
  • The leak that started an ecosystem A research-only model release leaked within a week, and the tooling that appeared in the following month showed there had been enormous unmet demand for weights anyone could run.

The one tight idea

You can adapt a large model by training a small number of extra numbers alongside the frozen originals, and if you also store the originals at lower precision the whole job fits on one ordinary machine.

2. What this lesson must cover

To write. Full prose, complete sentences, 1,400 to 2,000 words. Every bullet below is a beat the finished lesson owes the reader, in this order.

  • The idea in one sentence: freeze the model, add a small trainable correction beside it, and train only the correction.
  • Why a small correction is enough, stated at intuition level with the rank idea explained in words.
  • What it buys: far fewer trained numbers, much less memory, and adapters you can swap.
  • Quantization introduced only as far as this lesson needs it: fewer bits per number, and where the quality goes.
  • The combination that made single-machine fine-tuning ordinary.
Do not overclaim

Do not derive the low-rank decomposition. State the intuition, give one figure for the parameter reduction from the paper, and hand off.
