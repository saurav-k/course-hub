1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • When meaning became geometry Neural networks turned words from symbols that are either identical or unrelated into positions in a space where closeness means similarity, and that single change is what made everything after it possible.

The one tight idea

Translating a sentence by squeezing all of it into one fixed list of numbers works until the sentence gets long, and attention is the idea of not squeezing it at all.

4. Where this hands off

This course owns chronology, causation, people and dates. It hands off for mechanism, maths and code rather than re-deriving them.

  • Deep dive: Sequence to sequence, and the eight thousand numbers Read a whole sentence into a single fixed list of numbers and then write another sentence out of it, and that single list is both the elegance and the flaw.
  • Deep dive: Attention: stop compressing Do not squeeze the source sentence into one vector at all: keep every position, and at each output step learn a weighting that says which positions matter now.
  • Deep dive: The other problem: attention did not make it fast Attention fixed how far a model could reach, but the recurrence underneath it still forced one step per word, so training time grew with sentence length no matter how many machines you had.
  • Deep dive: Who was in the room: labs, money, and machines, 2014 to 2017 The institutions that would build large language models were founded, funded and already compute-constrained before the architecture that made them possible existed.
