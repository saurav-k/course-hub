Two papers, one day

On 12 June 2017 two unrelated papers were posted, one that made models fast and one that made them listen to people, and the rest of this course is those two threads converging.

Settled Accurate as of 21 August 2026. Interpretation is stable. Corrected by appending, never rewritten to fit a new event.
Where this lesson starts

This lesson is a hinge. It assumes you have felt the two problems that were open in mid-2017: models were slow to train, and there was no way to tell one what you wanted. It names the two answers and then hands off.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • The other problem: attention did not make it fast Attention fixed how far a model could reach, but the recurrence underneath it still forced one step per word, so training time grew with sentence length no matter how many machines you had.

The one tight idea

On 12 June 2017 two unrelated papers were posted, one that made models fast and one that made them listen to people, and the rest of this course is those two threads converging.
