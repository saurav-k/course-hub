Meaning as a direction in space

Give every word a position in a few hundred dimensions, learn those positions only from the company each word keeps, and similarity in meaning becomes closeness in space.

Settled Accurate as of 21 August 2026. Interpretation is stable. Corrected by appending, never rewritten to fit a new event.
Where this lesson starts

Counting treats every word as unrelated to every other word, so a model that has seen dog learns nothing about cat. This lesson is about the representation that fixed that, and it is where this course teaches vectors and dot products.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • What a neural network actually is A network is a stack of two operations, multiply by a table of numbers and then bend the result, and training is nudging those numbers until a measured error gets smaller.

The one tight idea

Give every word a position in a few hundred dimensions, learn those positions only from the company each word keeps, and similarity in meaning becomes closeness in space.

2. What this lesson must cover

To write. Full prose, complete sentences, 1,400 to 2,000 words. Every bullet below is a beat the finished lesson owes the reader, in this order.

  • The distributional hypothesis stated plainly: you know a word by the company it keeps.
  • What a vector is here, and what a dot product measures, taught inline with a worked two-dimensional example.
  • How word2vec learns: predict the neighbours, or predict the word from its neighbours.
  • GloVe's different route to the same place, from a global co-occurrence count.
  • The analogy result, presented carefully rather than as magic, including its known limitations.
  • Why this dissolves the curse of dimensionality: unseen combinations land near seen ones.
Do not overclaim

The king minus man plus woman result is real and it is also cherry-picked in most retellings. Report what the original papers measured and note that analogy accuracy is far from perfect.
