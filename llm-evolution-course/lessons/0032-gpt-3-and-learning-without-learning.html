GPT-3, and learning without learning

A model whose weights never change gets better at a task when you put a few worked examples in the prompt, which is a kind of learning that leaves no trace in the model.

Settled Accurate as of 21 August 2026. Interpretation is stable. Corrected by appending, never rewritten to fit a new event.
Where this lesson starts

Predicting the future with a straight line gives a rule for how big to build. This lesson is what happened when someone followed it, and the surprise that arrived with the result. It assumes you know that a trained model's weights are frozen.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • Predicting the future with a straight line Model loss falls as a power law in size, data and compute, which means a straight line on a log-log plot, which means you can forecast the result of a run you have not paid for.
  • Where the data actually came from The dataset behind GPT-2 was scraped by its authors and filtered by a proxy for human approval, it was not a public crawl, and that filtering idea outlived the model that introduced it.

The one tight idea

A model whose weights never change gets better at a task when you put a few worked examples in the prompt, which is a kind of learning that leaves no trace in the model.

2. What this lesson must cover

To write. Full prose, complete sentences, 1,400 to 2,000 words. Every bullet below is a beat the finished lesson owes the reader, in this order.

  • The build: size, training tokens, and why the scaling work made this the obvious next step.
  • In-context learning defined carefully: zero-shot, one-shot and few-shot as prompt formats, not training regimes.
  • Why it is surprising: nothing is being learned in the usual sense, since no weight moves.
  • Where it worked well and where it did not, from the paper's own tables.
  • The commercial turn: access through an interface rather than a download, and what that changed.
Do not overclaim

In-context learning is a description of a behaviour, not an explanation of it. Do not assert a mechanism for why it works.
