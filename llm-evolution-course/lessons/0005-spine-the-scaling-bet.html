The one tight idea

Between 2018 and 2022 the field discovered that the loss of a language model falls along a straight line as you spend more, which turned model building from a craft into a budgeting exercise, and then discovered the first version of that line was wrong.

  • Chinchilla: the first forecast held the wrong thing fixed, and data became the binding constraint.
Do not overclaim

A scaling law is an empirical fit over a measured range. It is not a law of nature and it does not promise anything outside that range.

4. Where this hands off

This course owns chronology, causation, people and dates. It hands off for mechanism, maths and code rather than re-deriving them.

  • Deep dive: GPT-2 and the bet on scale Make the decoder much bigger and train it on much more text, and the fine-tuning step starts to become unnecessary: you describe the task in the prompt instead.
  • Deep dive: Where the data actually came from The dataset behind GPT-2 was scraped by its authors and filtered by a proxy for human approval, it was not a public crawl, and that filtering idea outlived the model that introduced it.
  • Deep dive: The model they would not release The first time a lab withheld a trained model on the grounds that releasing it might be harmful, it set the terms of an argument the field is still having.
  • Deep dive: Predicting the future with a straight line Model loss falls as a power law in size, data and compute, which means a straight line on a log-log plot, which means you can forecast the result of a run you have not paid for.
  • Deep dive: GPT-3, and learning without learning A model whose weights never change gets better at a task when you put a few worked examples in the prompt, which is a kind of learning that leaves no trace in the model.
  • Deep dive: Chinchilla, the correction, and data as the new constraint The first scaling recipe held the wrong thing fixed, so the models built on it were far larger than they needed to be and far less trained than they should have been, and once that was corrected the scarce resource became text.
