Mixture of experts, part one: the router

Replace one large feed-forward network with many smaller ones and a small chooser that picks a couple per token, and what a model knows stops being the same number as what it costs to run.

Where this lesson starts

This lesson opens the central mechanism of the efficiency story. It assumes you know that a Transformer block contains a feed-forward network applied to each position, from The Transformer: what dropping recurrence unlocked.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • The Transformer: what dropping recurrence unlocked Delete the recurrence and keep only attention, and every position in a sentence can be computed at the same time with a constant number of operations between any two words.

The one tight idea

Replace one large feed-forward network with many smaller ones and a small chooser that picks a couple per token, and what a model knows stops being the same number as what it costs to run.

2. What this lesson must cover

To write. Full prose, complete sentences, 1,400 to 2,000 words. Every bullet below is a beat the finished lesson owes the reader, in this order.

  • Where the experts sit: the feed-forward part of the block, not the attention part.
  • The router: a small learned scorer that produces an affinity per expert for each token.
  • Top-k selection and the weighted recombination back into the residual stream.
  • Total parameters against active parameters, defined precisely and kept apart for the rest of the course.
  • One anchor model with both numbers stated and sourced.
Do not overclaim

Total and active parameter counts must both be quoted from the model's own card or paper. Do not compute one from the other.
