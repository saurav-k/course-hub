2012: the year the hardware caught up

The ideas that won in 2012 were roughly twenty-five years old, and what actually changed was a large labelled dataset and a graphics card that could do the arithmetic in parallel.

Settled Accurate as of 21 August 2026. Interpretation is stable. Corrected by appending, never rewritten to fit a new event.
Where this lesson starts

The methods in What a neural network actually is, Backpropagation, and the two winters and Sequences, memory, and the LSTM all existed by 1997 and did not take over. This lesson explains why they suddenly did, and it is about hardware and data rather than about mathematics.

1. Where this lesson sits

What you need first

  • What a neural network actually is A network is a stack of two operations, multiply by a table of numbers and then bend the result, and training is nudging those numbers until a measured error gets smaller.

The one tight idea

The ideas that won in 2012 were roughly twenty-five years old, and what actually changed was a large labelled dataset and a graphics card that could do the arithmetic in parallel.
