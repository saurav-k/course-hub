What did not work, and why that matters

The field expected the game-playing recipe of search plus a learned value function to transfer to language, two labs reported independently that it did not, and the simple recipe worked instead.

The one tight idea

The field expected the game-playing recipe of search plus a learned value function to transfer to language, two labs reported independently that it did not, and the simple recipe worked instead.

2. What this lesson must cover

To write. Full prose, complete sentences, 1,400 to 2,000 words. Every bullet below is a beat the finished lesson owes the reader, in this order.

  • The expectation, and why it was reasonable: the game-playing analogy was strong and widely believed.
  • What was tried and reported as unsuccessful, quoted from the papers' own sections rather than paraphrased.
  • Why search over language is harder than search over a board: the branching factor and the absence of a clean state.
  • The second lab's independent statement that it needed none of the machinery either.
  • Why a published negative result is worth as much here as a positive one, and how rare it is in this literature.
Do not overclaim

Reported as unsuccessful in one setting is not proven impossible. Say what was tried, by whom, at what scale, and what they concluded.
