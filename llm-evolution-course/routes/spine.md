# Route scope: Spine plus deep dives

**Route id:** `spine`
**Sections:** 10
**Pages:** 10 spine chapters and 47 deep dives

## What this route is

Ten short chapters that tell the whole story at low resolution, with no maths, readable in an evening.
Each chapter is followed by the deep dives that zoom into it.

The ten chapters are the only pages in the course that exist in no other route, and that is deliberate.
A low-resolution pass over the whole arc is not the same thing as any subset of the detailed lessons: it makes different choices about what to leave out, and it is written to be read straight through.

## The two ways to read it

- **The evening read.** The ten chapters and nothing else. Every chapter carries a **Chapter N of 10** line under its title with a direct link to the next chapter, so the evening read is a straight run of ten clicks. That line is static markup and works with scripting off.
- **The full read.** Straight down. Each chapter sets up its deep dives and the deep dives pay it off.

Both are supported and neither is preferred.
The course map for this route shows the chapters and their deep dives together, so the shape is visible before a reader commits.

## What it deliberately leaves out

- **Nothing.** This is the only route that covers every page in the pool, because it is the only one that carries the spine chapters as well as the deep dives.
- What each *chapter* leaves out is the point of a chapter: no maths, no mechanism beyond one sentence, no numbers that need a source. Anything a chapter has to skip is exactly what its deep dives are for.

## Who it is for

Someone new, and someone in a hurry.
It is the right first route for a reader who does not yet know whether they want the whole thing, and the right route to send to somebody else.

It is the wrong route for a reader who already knows the outline and wants causation, which is `constraint`, or dates, which is `era`.

## How to extend it

**A new deep dive goes under the chapter whose story it belongs to.**
If a new lesson does not obviously belong under one of the ten, that is a signal about the chapter structure and it is worth stopping to think about rather than forcing.

**A new chapter is a much bigger decision than a new deep dive.**
Ten chapters is a deliberate number: it is short enough that the evening read stays an evening.
Adding an eleventh is only justified when a genuinely new phase of the story exists, and it costs a rewrite of the chapters on either side of it, because a chapter's job is partly to hand over to the next one.

Rules for writing a chapter:

- No maths, and no formula of any kind.
- No number that needs a citation. If a chapter needs a number, the number belongs in a deep dive.
- End every chapter with the problem the next chapter opens on. The handover is what makes ten pages a story instead of ten summaries.
- A chapter names its deep dives and links them, which is what makes it an index as well as a narrative. The scaffold generates that list from the route manifest, so it stays complete on its own; do not hand-trim it.
- Keep a chapter to roughly a thousand words. If it grows past that, it is trying to be a deep dive.

Spine chapters are the pages most likely to need rewriting when the field moves, because they are the ones making the shape claims.
Chapters 08, 09 and 10 sit in the moving zone for that reason.
