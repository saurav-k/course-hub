Your staff and your customers are never the same directory

working 8 min Capabilities workforce-directory, workforce-sso, managed-directory, os-login and ciam-user-directory. Sourced from Workforce Identity Federation and Identity Platform.

The one-minute version

  • Cloud Identity is the standalone user directory, usable without Google Workspace, whose accounts and groups become IAM principals.
  • Workforce Identity Federation trusts an external identity provider so staff get direct access without syncing anyone into Cloud Identity, with attributes mapped into principals usable in IAM bindings.
  • Managed Service for Microsoft Active Directory runs real domain controllers with LDAP, Kerberos and domain join, and can trust an existing on-premises forest rather than mirroring it.
  • OS Login ties Linux SSH authorisation to IAM, with optional two-factor and SSH certificates. The alternative is metadata-key SSH.
  • Identity Platform is the customer directory: multi-tenant, with email, phone, anonymous and custom auth, social and enterprise federation, and SMS or app-based second factors.

1. The split, drawn once

2. Staff: sync them, or trust somebody else's directory

Cloud Identity is the other shape: a directory of your own, usable without Workspace, with a free edition, whose accounts and groups become principals directly. And where workloads need Windows-native authentication, Managed Service for Microsoft AD runs actual domain controllers as a highly available pair per domain, reachable over an authorised VPC network. Because it is real Active Directory rather than a projection, it can trust an existing on-premises forest.

3. Reaching a machine, which is a third question

4. Customers, and the cost that is easy to miss

Two things in this area are delivered elsewhere rather than as their own products, and both are easy to look for and not find. There is no separate directory audit stream inside the cloud - Cloud Identity administrative and sign-in events are recorded in the admin console audit logs and reach Cloud Logging as Admin Activity records, which module 10 covers. And there is no single conditional-access policy engine: the capability is assembled from Access Context Manager access levels applied through IAM conditions and Identity-Aware Proxy, which module 11 covers.
The reframing Ask which authorisation system decides. If the answer is IAM, the person is workforce, and the choice is between holding the directory yourself and federating to one you already have. If the answer is your application, the person is a customer, and Identity Platform is the directory. A design that puts customers into the first branch has just made every customer an IAM principal, which is a governance problem long before it is a technical one.

Check yourself

Retrieval practice

Answer from memory before revealing.

What does Workforce Identity Federation remove that a directory sync does not?
Federation gives direct access without syncing anyone, which is the operational win. The second moves authorisation into the identity provider, which is not what mapping does. The third removes sign-in entirely. The fourth would exempt a principal from the chain, which nothing does.
Which directory product runs real Microsoft domain controllers?
It runs actual Active Directory, which is why it supports domain join and can trust an on-premises forest. The first and second attribute Windows-native protocols to directories that do not run them. The third federates to a provider rather than running a domain.
A consumer application will have millions of end users. Where do their identities belong?
Customers belong in the customer directory, and their authorisation is your application's rather than IAM's. The other three all place end users on the workforce branch, and each would make millions of application users into infrastructure principals.

Practice about 15 minutes, with paper

Practice 1working

A company already runs an enterprise identity provider for its 4,000 staff. It is building a customer portal whose users sign in with an email address or a social account, and some corporate customers want their own employees federated in. A Windows application being migrated needs domain join. Engineers need SSH to Linux instances, and a recent leaver kept access for a fortnight.

Place each population, choose a machine-access design and justify it with the leaver, and say which requirement crosses the two branches and how it is still not one directory.

Hint

Four requirements, and the branch question sorts three of them immediately. The fourth mentions federation on the customer side, which sounds like the staff answer and is not.

Show the worked solution
  1. Place the staff. The identity provider already exists, so Workforce Identity Federation. Nobody is synchronised into Cloud Identity, and joiners and leavers keep being handled in the one directory that already handles them.
  2. Place the customers. Identity Platform, with email and social sign-in. Their authorisation is the portal's, not IAM's, so none of them becomes a Google Cloud principal.
  3. Place the Windows application. Managed Service for Microsoft AD. Domain join needs real Active Directory, and this is the product that runs it, reachable over an authorised VPC network.
  4. Design machine access. OS Login, so SSH authorisation is IAM. The leaver is the argument: with OS Login, removing the person from the federated group removes the access in one operation. With metadata-key SSH, revocation means finding keys wherever they were placed, which is why a fortnight passed.
  5. Answer the crossing requirement. The corporate customers wanting their own employees federated. That is enterprise federation into Identity Platform, which supports SAML and OIDC providers per tenant. It looks like the staff answer and is not - those employees are still customers of the portal, and they still never become IAM principals.

Sanity check. Exactly two of your five answers should produce IAM principals, and the corporate-customer employees should not be among them. If they ended up in the workforce pool, count how many external companies would then hold identities inside your cloud directory.

Your teacher. When a requirement says "let them log in", ask an agent one question first: which system will decide what they are allowed to do. IAM and your application are different answers, and they select different products before any feature is compared.

Primary source to go deeper

Workforce Identity Federation is the reference for federated staff access without a sync. Identity Platform covers the customer directory, its tenants and its federation, and OS Login covers IAM-governed SSH.
