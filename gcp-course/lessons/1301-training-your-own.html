Training is one of six pieces, and not the one that decides

working 7 min Capability model-training-platform. Sourced from the Vertex AI platform introduction.

The one-minute version

  • The platform is six pieces: custom training jobs, hyperparameter tuning, pipelines, a feature store, a model registry and endpoints.
  • The two that decide whether a model is ever reproducible are the feature store and the registry, and neither of them trains anything.
  • Training runs on GPU and TPU pools, with Spot options - the same reclaimable capacity taught in module 02, and the same bargain.
  • Pipelines are what turn a notebook result into something that runs again, which is the step most teams skip and then repeat by hand.
  • An endpoint is a serving decision like any other runtime choice, and it is where a trained model starts costing money continuously rather than in bursts.

The reframing Judge a machine-learning effort by which pieces it has adopted rather than by its model. Training and tuning mean somebody produced a result. Pipelines mean it can happen again. A feature store and a registry mean it can be trusted and traced. An endpoint means it is serving, and paying. The order in that list is also the order in which most teams stop.

Retrieval practice

Answer from memory before revealing.

A model performs well in evaluation and badly in production on apparently similar inputs. Which piece addresses that class of problem?
A feature computed one way for training and another way at request time is the classic cause, and one definition used by both is the fix. The first treats a data problem as a modelling one. The third records versions without changing what they are fed. The fourth invents a hardware dependency.
What do pipelines add that a notebook does not?
Repeatability is the contribution, and it is the step most efforts skip. The first invents a restriction. The second describes the registry. The third invents a default the inventory does not record.
Which training jobs suit Spot capacity?
Reclaimable capacity suits work that can resume, which is what checkpointing provides. The second and third both claim reclamation behaviour the inventory does not record. The fourth applies interruptible capacity to serving, which is where an interruption is most visible.

Practice about 12 minutes, with paper

Practice 1working

A data science team has a model in a notebook that scores well. They want it in production next month. They have no feature definitions outside the notebook, no record of which experiment produced the current weights, and a large monthly compute budget they are worried about.

List what they must adopt before this is production, say which single gap would cause the most damage if ignored, and name the lever on their compute worry and the condition it requires.

Hint

Two of the six pieces are missing entirely and both are in the middle pair. The cost lever comes from module 02 and has a prerequisite in the code.

Show the worked solution
  1. Pipelines. The sequence has to be expressed so it runs again without the notebook and without the person. Until that exists, every retrain is a manual reproduction of something nobody wrote down.
  2. A feature store. Feature definitions live only in the notebook today, so nothing guarantees that the value served at request time is computed the way the training value was.
  3. The model registry. No record exists of which experiment produced the current weights, so "roll back to the previous model" has no meaning and neither does an audit question about what was serving last week.
  4. Name the most damaging gap. The feature store. The other two make the effort slow and untraceable; this one makes the model quietly wrong in production while continuing to score well in evaluation, which is the failure nobody notices for months.
  5. Name the cost lever. Spot capacity on the training jobs, from module 02. Its condition is checkpointing - a job that cannot resume after a worker is reclaimed cannot take the bargain, so the code change comes before the saving.

Sanity check. Your list should contain three pieces and only one of them should do any training. If the compute answer did not name a code prerequisite, note that reclaimable capacity is only cheap for a job that survives being interrupted.

Your teacher. Ask an agent which of the six pieces a project is actually using. The answer is usually the first two and the last one, and the missing middle pair is what turns a promising model into something nobody can reproduce or explain.
