# Mission

## Who
Saurav — total beginner to transformer/LLM internals, wants to genuinely *understand* Large Language Models from first principles, not just use them.

## Why
To read the 37 foundational LLM research papers *in the right order* and come out able to explain **and implement** the core mechanisms of modern LLMs. The goal is deep, durable understanding — mechanism-level, not vibes.

## The concrete goal
A **publishable course**: 38 self-contained HTML lessons (1 primer + 37 papers), ranked easiest → hardest, each with:
- Rich diagrams (a *lot* of them) — block diagrams, sequence diagrams, flow charts.
- Everything explained in plain grammatical English before any math.
- Deep practical implementation — runnable code, training loops, benchmarks/ablations where relevant.
- Retrieval-practice quiz + a recommended primary source.

The full set is meant to be shipped as an online course.

## Success looks like
Saurav can, for any of the 37 papers: (1) say *why the paper exists* / what problem it solves, (2) draw the mechanism, (3) explain the math in words, (4) implement the core idea in code, (5) place it in the lineage of the other papers.

## Style constraints
- No em dashes (use plain `-`).
- Diagrams everywhere. Grammatically explainable everything.
- Beginner-friendly on-ramp, implementation depth on tap.
- Consistent look across all lessons so it reads as ONE course.
